## 2. Abstract

Flavor is an embodied, multimodal, and culturally situated design material. However, computational flavor systems often represent food through ingredients, chemical compounds, recipe corpora, or machine-generated recommendations, leaving limited space for people to document and iterate their subjective experiences.

Bobapedia is a full-day participatory workshop exploring how computational interfaces scaffold collaborative flavor creation through a popular drink: boba tea. Using an LLM-supported Flavor Wiki, participants will record sensory impressions, personal and cultural associations, and flavor interpretations before collaboratively designing customized drinks.

The workshop will produce a collaboratively edited Flavor Wiki containing annotated ingredient entries and boba-tea concepts that capture not only recipes, but also the sensory, cultural, and narrative reasoning behind them.

## 3. Workshop motivation

The workshop asks:

> How might computational interfaces help communities articulate, share, and iteratively refine their personal interpretations of taste?

Boba tea is used as the workshop medium because it combines cultural meaning with extensive customization, including sweetness, ice level, milk type, tea base, and toppings.

## 4. What participants will do

Participants will:

1. Taste and smell assigned ingredients.
2. Describe sensory attributes, personal associations, and cultural references.
3. Record their experiences in the Flavor Wiki.
4. Work in groups to compare flavor concepts.
5. Design boba tea around an ingredient, memory, or theme.
6. Prepare and taste the resulting drinks.
7. Annotate and discuss other groups' flavor entries and creations.

No culinary skills are required.

## 11. Expected outcomes

The workshop will produce:

- A collaboratively edited Flavor Wiki;
- Annotated ingredient entries;
- Boba-tea concepts and recipes;
- Sensory, cultural, and narrative explanations of design decisions;
- Shared discussion about LLM normalization and idiosyncratic flavor expressions;
- A research agenda connecting computational flavor models with embodied experience.

The organizers plan to publish selected workshop materials on the website, subject to participant consent and privacy considerations.
